# Privacy Policy — Dawrati (دورتي)

_Last updated: 13 July 2026_

Dawrati ("the App", "we", "us") is a menstrual cycle tracking application. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

## Data we collect

**Stored on your device only** (unless you sign in):
- Cycle start date and cycle history
- Daily logs: mood, symptoms, water intake, sleep, weight, medications, notes
- App settings: language, notification preferences, App Lock PIN (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text)

**Synced to our cloud servers (Firebase/Google Cloud), only if you sign in:**
- Your email address (used for authentication)
- The cycle and daily log data listed above, kept in sync across your devices
- Your subscription status
- Referral codes and credits, if you use the referral program
- A limited "partner view" snapshot (cycle phase, mood, hydration, sleep, next period/ovulation dates) if you enable Partner Mode and share your code with someone

**Sent to our AI provider (OpenAI), only when you use AI Coach or Symptom Checker features:**
- Relevant context from your cycle and logs needed to generate a personalized response
- Your chat messages to the AI coach
- This data is used solely to generate the response and is not used by us or the AI provider to train models on your identity

**Payment information:**
- Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card details.

## What we don't do

- We do not sell your data to third parties.
- We do not use your health data for advertising.
- Dawrati is not a diagnostic tool and does not provide medical advice.

## Your choices

- You can use most features without creating an account — your data then stays only on your device.
- You can delete all locally stored data at any time from Settings → Privacy → "Clear my data."
- You can sign out at any time; your cloud data remains associated with your account until you request deletion (contact us to request full account and data deletion).

## Data retention

We retain your cloud data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or request deletion, we remove your data within a reasonable timeframe.

## Children's privacy

Dawrati is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your local law for menstrual tracking).

## Contact

Questions about this policy or data requests: **support@dawrati.app**

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
